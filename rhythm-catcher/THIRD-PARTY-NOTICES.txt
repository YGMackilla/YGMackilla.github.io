RHYTHM CATCHER
Credits and third-party notices

Rhythm Catcher was conceived and built between 1 October and November 2026. Its design, code, music
arrangements, text and artwork were made for this game. The parts listed below came from other
people's work and are used under the licences shown.


MUSIC

The eighteen songs are public-domain melodies: traditional tunes long in print, and pieces by
composers who died more than a century ago. The arrangements and the backing band were made for
this game. No recording by anyone else is used.

   1  Korobeiniki             Russian folk song; words from a poem of 1861
   2  Twinkle, Twinkle        French melody "Ah! vous dirai-je, maman", published 1761
   3  Fur Elise               Ludwig van Beethoven, 1810
   4  Happy Birthday          Melody "Good Morning to All", Mildred and Patty Hill, published 1893
   5  Can-Can                 Jacques Offenbach, "Orpheus in the Underworld", 1858
   6  Ode to Joy              Ludwig van Beethoven, Symphony No. 9, 1824
   7  Turkish March           W. A. Mozart, Piano Sonata No. 11, published 1784
   8  Frere Jacques           French nursery tune, eighteenth century
   9  Jingle Bells            James Lord Pierpont, 1857
  10  Yankee Doodle           Traditional, eighteenth century
  11  When the Saints         Traditional American spiritual
  12  The Entertainer         Scott Joplin, 1902
  13  Canon in D              Johann Pachelbel, about 1700
  14  Oh! Susanna             Stephen Foster, 1848
  15  Mountain King           Edvard Grieg, "In the Hall of the Mountain King", 1875
  16  Up She Rises            The sea shanty "Drunken Sailor", traditional, in print since the 1840s
  17  Eine kleine Nachtmusik  W. A. Mozart, 1787
  18  Korobeiniki, Complete   As 1


INSTRUMENT SOUNDS

The melody notes are samples from the FluidR3 GM SoundFont by Frank Wen and contributors, licensed
under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported licence:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

They were taken as individual notes from the MIDI.js Soundfonts project
(https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts) and changed for this game: reduced to one channel,
resampled to 22.05 kHz, shortened with a fade, and re-encoded as MP3: 212 notes in all, across the
game's eighteen instrument voices.
