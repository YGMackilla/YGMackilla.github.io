How to play
  1. Pinch thumb and finger together to catch a firefly and its note.
  2. Wide gold ring? Pinch and keep holding until the ring closes.
  3. Tethered pairs hold a note each and need one hand apiece.
  4. In a headset, turn a palm toward you for Pause and Settings, and tap them with your other hand.
  5. Miss one and its note stays silent when the book sings.

No install: open this page in your headset's browser and press Enter VR — the game plays with your bare hands. Reviewing on a screen? Play in 2D: the mouse moves your hand, hold the left button to pinch, and the arrow keys look around. On a phone, one finger moves your hand and pinches with it, and two fingers turn your view.

Settings

These choices change the way the garden meets you. None of them starts a song.

Sound Turn the garden and song sounds on or off.
Note names The note name floats over each firefly.
Timing assist A wider window for Perfect and Good, so slower hands still finish the song.
Reduced motion Drifting petals, snow, weather and camera movement stop.
High contrast Colours and outlines grow stronger.
Larger text Cards and labels grow larger, so less fits at once.
One-hand pairs Pinch, open, then pinch its partner for tethered pairs.
